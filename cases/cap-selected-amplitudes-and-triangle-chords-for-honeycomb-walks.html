A strict comparison becomes an equality

Section 9 · Proof of Proposition 9.1

Issue & evidence

The final strict comparison in lines 3055–3057 is false when Q≥ζ: with q=max(Q,ζ) and ζ>7.5, both Q(0.024/q+0.012/q) and 0.036 min(1,Q/7.5) equal 0.036. This occurs within the stated first-atom domain, for example at b=4/5 and x=1/10, where Q=8>ζ. The error concerns the intermediate comparison; the intended strict total-variation bound remains valid.

Proposed repair by OSVerify

Replace the chain in lines 3055–3057 by Q TV_[q,∞)(h_-/u)≤3mQ/q<0.036 min(1,Q/7.5), retaining the exact maximum m from line 2984. The product-variation inequality gives the first bound because TV_[q,∞)(h_-)≤2m, sup h_-≤m, and TV_[q,∞)(1/u)=sup(1/u)=1/q. The strict inequality follows from m<0.012 and Q/q≤min(1,Q/7.5). This preserves the subsequent absolute-variation estimates in lines 3061–3063 and 3103–3109 and their contributions to the edge-deficit bounds.
