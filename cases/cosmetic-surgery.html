Minor · Issue 01Link ↗

The single-interval case misstates how many parameters disappear

Section 5.4 · Lemma 5.8

Issue & evidence

In the single-interval k=1 case at line 4916, “both parameters disappear” misstates the parameter count: there is only one interval parameter. Its removal leaves both resulting pieces with p=0, consistent with the displayed parameter total and the bounds in lines 4918–4920.

Proposed repair by OSVerify

Replace “both parameters disappear” with “the single interval parameter disappears, so both pieces have p=0.” This correction preserves the test assignments and the subsequent single-interval score calculations.

Locations in the paper

Section 5.3; Proof of Proposition 5.7; Section 5.4; Lemma 5.8; Proof of Lemma 5.8; Equation (5.19).

Minor · Issue 02Link ↗

The index equality counts virtually restored lens parameters

Section 9.7 · Proof of Proposition 9.12

Issue & evidence

The actual-index equality in lines 9831–9832 counts virtually restored lens parameters as actual parameters. With h_i separated lens ends, the open-face pure-line index before tests and particle-position variables is d_s+p_i-h_i: p_i restores those h_i parameters, while the sharp fillings contribute zero tangential correction. This overcount does not invalidate the subsequent upper bound.

Proposed repair by OSVerify

Replace “has index d_s+p_i” with “has index at most d_s+p_i,” explaining the loss of one parameter per separated lens end. Including particle positions gives index at most d_s+p_i-h_i+4ℓ_i≤d_s+p_i+4ℓ_i. Nonemptiness and tangential transversality therefore still imply the required inequality, preserving its use in the outside-component and fixed-type exclusions.

Locations in the paper

Section 9.7; Proof of Proposition 9.12; Section 8.3; Section 8.2; Proposition 8.3; Definition 8.7; Equation (8.14).

Minor · Issue 03Link ↗

The boundary-flux pairing has its sign reversed

Section 9.4

Issue & evidence

The boundary-flux evaluation at line 9167 has its sign reversed. For Q=G*G with limiting operator −∂t²+Δγ, Green's boundary expression is ⟨∂tξ,χ_e c⟩−⟨ξ,∂t(χ_e c)⟩. With ξ=a_e+t b_e plus a decaying remainder and t increasing outward, its limit is +⟨b_e,c⟩. The conclusion b_e=0 is unaffected.

Correction requested by OSVerify

Replace −⟨b_e,c⟩_{L²(Y_e)} by +⟨b_e,c⟩_{L²(Y_e)} at line 9167. Since the pairing is zero for every independently allowed parallel end constant c, this correction retains b_e=0 and the same starting point for the subsequent integration-by-parts argument.

Locations in the paper

Section 9.4.
