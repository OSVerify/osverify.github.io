Silverman’s Chapter X results are mislabeled

Where the issue occurs

Section 5, proof of Proposition 2.1, citation in the two-isogeny descent paragraph · p. 44

[67, Chapter X, Proposition 4.7 and Example 4.9, pp. 336–337]

The citation mislabels the results: 4.7 is a remark and 4.9 is a proposition.

Issue & evidence

The citation in the quoted descent paragraph mislabels Silverman's Chapter X results: 4.7 is a remark, and 4.9 is a proposition. This is a citation-label error and does not invalidate the descent application.

Proposed repair by OSVerify

Replace the locator in the quoted descent paragraph with: Chapter X, Remark 4.7, Example 4.8, Proposition 4.9, and Remark 4.9.1, pp. 335–338. These cover the quotient sequence, two-isogeny formulas, square-class map, and dual application used here.
