Silverman’s Chapter X results are mislabeled

Section 5 · Proof of Proposition 2.1

Issue & evidence

The citation at line 3372 mislabels Silverman's Chapter X results: 4.7 is a remark, and 4.9 is a proposition. This is a citation-label error and does not invalidate the descent application.

Proposed repair by OSVerify

Replace the locator at line 3372 with: Chapter X, Remark 4.7, Example 4.8, Proposition 4.9, and Remark 4.9.1, pp. 335–338. These cover the quotient sequence, two-isogeny formulas, square-class map, and dual application used here.
