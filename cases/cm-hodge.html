Milne’s article has the wrong page range

Where the issue occurs

References, entry [25], Milne, “Lefschetz motives and the Tate conjecture” · p. 52

J. S. Milne. Lefschetz motives and the Tate conjecture. Compositio Mathematica, 117(1):45–76, 1999.

The erroneous part is the page range 45–76; the published range is 47–81.

Issue & evidence

The Milne1999Tate bibliography entry gives pages 45–76, but the published range is 47–81, as confirmed by Milne’s article page. This is a nonblocking bibliographic error.

Proposed repair by OSVerify

Change the Milne1999Tate bibliography entry to pages = {47--81}.
