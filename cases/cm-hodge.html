Milne’s article has the wrong page range

Bibliography, entry “Milne1999Tate”

Issue & evidence

The Milne1999Tate bibliography entry gives pages 45–76, but the published range is 47–81, as confirmed by Milne’s article page. This is a nonblocking bibliographic error.

Correction requested by OSVerify

Change the Milne1999Tate pages field to pages = {47--81}. The existing theorem and remark locators need no adjustment for this pagination correction.
