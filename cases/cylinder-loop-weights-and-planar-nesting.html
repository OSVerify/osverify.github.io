The global kernel bound omits a bulk contribution

Section 3.6 · Proof of Lemma 3.9

Issue & evidence

The unqualified C/d(s) integral estimate in lines 2493–2494 is too strong for the full second kernel R(s,v)=θ′(s)θ′(v)/sin²((θ(s)+θ(v))/2). Its integral over v∈[−A,A] equals 4θ′(s)/sinθ(s). At s=0 this tends to 4πvarrho(0)>0, whereas C/d(0) tends to zero. The endpoint estimate is valid locally, but a global estimate needs a bounded bulk contribution.

Proposed repair by OSVerify

The exact integral 4θ′(s)/sinθ(s), together with θ′=πλ_L and sinθ(s) comparable to λ_L(s)min(1,d(s)), gives the corrected global bound C(1+1/d(s)). By symmetry of R, this yields ∫∫R(s,v)|Q(s)+Q(v)|²dsdv≤C(‖Q‖₂²+∫_{d(s)≤2}|Q(s)|²/d(s)ds). Thus the existing L² term absorbs the bulk contribution, preserving the upper comparison in line 2478 and its use in the subsequent gap and counting argument.
