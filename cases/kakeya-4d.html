Minor · Issue 01Link ↗

The weighted plank estimate has the wrong lemma number

Section 1.1 · Section 2.5 · Section 5.1

Issue & evidence

The cited September 23, 2026 preprint states the weighted plank estimate as Lemma 3.3 on page 20; item 2.3 is instead the critical-exponent definition. The incorrect locator appears at candidate lines 200, 1366, 5751, and 13753. The intended lemma's statement matches the weighted plank input.

Proposed repair by OSVerify

Replace Lemma~2.3 with Lemma~3.3 in the three citations to ThreeDimensional and its bibliography note. This corrects the reference without altering the stated weighted plank estimate.

Locations in the paper

Section 1.1; Section 2.5; Proof of Lemma 2.15; Section 5.1; Proof of Lemma 5.1; Bibliography, entry “ThreeDimensional”.

Minor · Issue 02Link ↗

The Jacobian sentence reverses the coordinate maps

Section 4.3 · Proof of Proposition 4.7 · Equation (4.20)

Issue & evidence

The Jacobian sentence preceding (4.20) reverses the coordinate maps. The parent-to-unit coordinate map base_Q has absolute Jacobian (q_sJ_s)⁻¹; q_sJ_s belongs to its inverse. The displayed pushforward density estimate uses the correct change-of-variables factor.

Proposed repair by OSVerify

Replace the Jacobian sentence with: “Writing u = base_Q(t,y) for unit base coordinates, the volume elements satisfy dt dy = q_sJ_s du.” Keep (4.20) and the following time-normalization formulas unchanged; their change-of-variables factors are already correct.

Locations in the paper

Section 2.3; Definition 2.8; Equation (2.7); Section 2.4; Proof of Lemma 2.13; Equation (2.15); Section 4.3; Proof of Proposition 4.7; Equation (4.20).
